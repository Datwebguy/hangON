The problem

Mike is under a sink.
His phone rings.

He can't answer. The caller hangs up and books the next plumber. That job is gone.

How HangON works

It handles the whole call, like a real receptionist.

01

Answers

Picks up, listens, and asks what's wrong.

02

Understands

Keeps the caller's final answer, even when they change their mind.

03

Books

Checks the real calendar and books only after the caller says yes.

04

Follows up

Emails the caller and gives the technician a brief.
